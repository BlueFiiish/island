Read this first

These win rates are not centred on 50%. The source tracks one high-MMR side per match, so the whole pool runs high — the overall median is 53.8%. A 52% god is below average here. Every row prints its gap to the median of its own scope, and the bands are cut from that gap, never from an absolute line.

Roles are separate pools: a god can be S in Solo and C overall. Rows under 100 matches carry a small sample badge and should be treated as noise, not signal.
