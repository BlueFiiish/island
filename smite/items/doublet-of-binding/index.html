Doublet of Binding

Mag ProtHealth

Cost 2,550

When to build

Niche peel-focused support item for guardians who want to keep a hyper-carry alive in long fights. Build it as a 4th/5th item after your core protections are up — the active only shines if you have meaningful mitigation. Skip vs heavy burst comps where the carry dies before the link matters; Stampede or Phantom Veil save them better. Best vs poke/sustained DPS where 20% redirect = lots of saved HP over a fight.
