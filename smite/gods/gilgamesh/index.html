Gilgamesh

Babylonian | King of Uruk

PASSIVE

Epic of Gilgamesh

You wield artifacts that demand quests to unlock their true potential

  • Sun-forged Scimitar: 5 Successful Attacks against gods while ignited 3 times
  • Drop Kick: Drop Kick a target into a god 3 times
  • Hero's Advance: Kill or Assist 3 gods affected by Hero's Advance damage bonus
  • Winds of Shamash: Knockback a target off the wall 5 times
  • Each Quest: 100 gold + upgrade

ABILITY

Sun-forged Scimitar

Ignite your sword for 3 seconds. Enemies around the ignition take Physical Damage and are Slowed

  • Lane minions take increased damage
  • Your Attacks deal bonus Physical Damage based on your Max Health and extend the effect per hit
  • You gain Health Regen per rank
  • Quest Upgrade: Initial ignition extends buff on enemy hits; max duration +3 seconds
  • Duration can be extended up to 6 seconds by attacks
  • Empowered attacks count as two hits on wards and player-made deployables

ABILITY

Drop Kick

Drop kick an area in front of you, dealing Physical Damage to enemies. The closest enemy is Knocked Forward, taking bonus Physical Damage when hitting a minion or burst Physical Damage and being Stunned when hitting a god or wall. Launched enemies hitting Winds of Shamash are Knocked Back towards the center of the ring

  • Enemies Knocked Forward pass through minions, but stop on walls and enemy gods
  • Quest Upgrade: When Stun fades, targets are Intoxicated
  • Player-made walls also trigger the burst damage and Stun

ABILITY

Hero's Advance

Leap to a target location, dealing Physical Damage on impact. Create a beacon for 5 seconds

  • Center sweet spot applies increased Damage to enemies
  • Allies moving toward the beacon gain Movement Speed, halved once inside
  • Full Movement Speed buff range is 17.6 meters
  • Quest Upgrade: The beacon now travels with you

ABILITY

Winds of Shamash

Call the winds of Shamash at a target location for 6 seconds. Enemies inside at start take Physical Damage and are Slowed. Enemies in the ring take Physical Damage every 0.5 seconds. Outer wall heavily Slows; your damage Knocks Back enemies towards the center. At 6 seconds, wind becomes thread, dealing bonus Physical Damage and Rooting enemies inside

  • Quest Upgrade: Ticks every 0.375 seconds and the outer wall Slow increases to 70%

BASIC ATTACK

Gilgamesh Basic Attack

Deal Physical Damage to an enemy in front of you

  • If multiple enemies are in the area, the enemy closest to the center of the area will be hit
