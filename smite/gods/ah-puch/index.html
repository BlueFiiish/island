Ah Puch

Maya | Horrific God of Decay

PASSIVE

Hollow Ground

Exhume decaying corpses at will. When you walk over a decaying corpse, reduce the Cooldown of Undead Surge and Corpse Explosion, and restore a portion of your max Health and Mana

  • Enemy gods and the Fire Giant can destroy corpses with 2 Attacks
  • You can have up to 6 corpses; placing more removes the oldest
  • Corpses last up to 45 seconds

ABILITY

Undead Surge

Raise two decaying corpses from the underworld that surge forward, dealing Magical Damage. Enemies hit are Slowed and have Reduced Healing for a duration

  • Reduces enemy Healing
  • Corpses explode on contact, at max range, or when reactivating Undead Surge
  • Corpses collapse after exploding and remain behind
  • This ability stops on first target hit, and does not pass through walls

ABILITY

Corpse Explosion

Cause the target area to explode, dealing Magical Damage

  • Any corpses in the area detonate, dealing additional Magical Damage

ABILITY

Fleeting Breath

Throw a charm from the underworld that awakens a decaying corpse, applying miasma to enemies in an area, dealing Magical Damage over time

  • Enemies that Heal while affected by miasma take bonus Magical Damage and are Stunned at the end of its effect
  • The corpse falls to the ground and remains behind

ABILITY

Empty the Crypts

Unleash a field of spirits at a target location for 6 seconds. Enemies inside the field take Magical Damage every 0.5 seconds

  • Each tick applies stacking Healing Reduction and Damage Dealt Reduction (stacks refresh)
  • After casting this ability, your next 3 Attacks also launch zombies that leave decaying corpses behind

BASIC ATTACK

Ah Puch Basic Attack

Fire a projectile that deals Magical Damage to the first enemy hit

  • Projectile stops on first target hit, and does not pass through walls
