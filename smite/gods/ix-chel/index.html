PASSIVE

Rainbow Weaver

Gain a Rainbow Weaver stack whenever your Abilities hit an enemy god or grant a beneficial effect to an allied god. At 6 stacks, your next Attack has no movement penalty, is larger, travels farther, and deals bonus Damage

  • The empowered Attack is spent even if it misses
  • The empowered Attack triggers item effects and it benefits from 33% Lifesteal
  • The empowered Attack has 10% increased projectile speed and can hit enemy structures from beyond their attack radius

Gleaming Blast

Fire a blast of gleaming blast light that deals Magical Damage, stopping on the first enemy god hit and transforming this ability into Threads of Light

  • After Gleaming Blast hits an enemy, Threads of Light can be cast immediately
  • This ability does 40% bonus damage to Minions and Jungle Monsters
  • This ability passes through and damages non-god enemies, and passes through walls, but stops on enemy gods

ABILITY

Lunar Invocation

Invoke lunar energy at an area. After a brief delay, it explodes, dealing Magical Damage and Knocking Up enemies while Healing allies

ABILITY

Rainbow Crash

Fire an arcing rainbow and gain Movement Speed. When it lands, deal Magical Damage and Root enemies. The rainbow splits into orbs that you and allied gods can pick up to gain Movement Speed

  • You can benefit from the Movement Speed of both the initial cast and an orb
  • Orbs bounce off walls a maximum of 2 times. Each allied god can pick up only 1 orb per cast

ABILITY

Great End

Become CC Immune and channel a rainbow beam. Enemies repeatedly take Magical Damage and allied gods repeatedly Heal. Damaging an enemy god or Healing an allied god also Heals you

  • Simultaneously hitting an enemy god and allied god extends the channel
  • Repeated hits deal increasing damage; this bonus rapidly decays when hits stop
  • Firing the beam grants maximum Rainbow Weaver stacks
  • If you start the Ability with maximum Rainbow Weaver stacks, gain bonus Echo for the duration
