Chaac

Maya | God of Rain

PASSIVE

Overflow

After 5 successful Basic Attacks, the next ability you use costs 0 mana, has Lower Cooldown, and Heals you

ABILITY

Thunder Strike

Throw your Axe forward, dealing Physical Damage to enemies in the area where it lands

  • The Axe remains deployed to empower Torrent or Rain Dance
  • Reactivate to destroy the Axe early

ABILITY

Torrent

Swing your Axe around you, dealing Physical Damage to enemies and gaining Protections for each enemy hit

  • If the Axe is deployed, first Dash to it and deal Physical Damage to enemies that you pass through
  • This Dash passes through walls
  • If you Dash, you will still do the area attack upon reaching the Axe
  • Minions provide 1 stack, gods provide 2 stacks

ABILITY

Rain Dance

Create a rainstorm that Heals you over time and provides Dampening

  • Enemies caught in the rain are Slowed and have Reduced Attack Speed
  • Heals 8 times over 6 seconds
  • If the Axe is deployed, create a Rain Storm at the Axe's location as well
  • Enemies in both Rain Storms are Slowed and Debuffed for twice as much
  • Grants a stack of Overflow every 1.5s

ABILITY

Storm Call

Charge up your Lightning Axe, then unleash a blast dealing Physical Damage, Knocking Up, and Silencing in an area around you

  • You are CC Immune and gain Damage Mitigation while Channeling
  • Enemies are knocked straight up

BASIC ATTACK

Chaac Basic Attack

Deal Physical Damage to an enemy in front of you

  • If multiple enemies are in the area, the enemy closest to the center of the area will be hit
  • Has a 3 hit chain. Attacks in order of 1, 0.5, 1.5x damage and swing time
