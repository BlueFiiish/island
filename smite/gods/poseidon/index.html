Poseidon

Greek | God of the Oceans

PASSIVE

Changing Tides

Successfully hit Attacks to gain Tide. You have increased Movement Speed and your abilities do additional damage based on the amount of Tide you have.

  • Each successful Attack generates 10% Tide
  • Using abilities decreases Tide

ABILITY

Tidal Surge

Summon a wave, dealing Magical Damage and Knocking Back enemies. Activate while Trident is active to send out two additional waves

  • Subsequent wave hits deal 50% less damage
  • This ability passes through and damages all enemies, and passes through walls

ABILITY

Trident

Activate your Trident, gaining increased Attack Speed, Movement Speed, and Attack Damage. While activated Tidal Surge and Attacks fire two additional shots

  • The additional Attack shots apply ability damage and trigger ability on-hit effects
  • All Attack shots increase Tide on successful hit
  • This ability does not cost Tide

ABILITY

Whirlpool

Summon a whirlpool, Crippling, Vortexing, and dealing Magical Damage repeatedly to enemies in an area

  • This ability hits 6 times over 3 seconds

ABILITY

Release the Kraken!

Summon the Kraken, dealing Magical Damage and Slowing enemies in its area. Enemies in the center area when it spawns take increased Magical Damage and are Stunned and Knocked Up

BASIC ATTACK

Poseidon Basic Attack

Fire a projectile that deals Magical Damage to the first enemy hit

  • Projectile stops on first target hit, and does not pass through walls
  • Successfully hit Attacks generate Tide
