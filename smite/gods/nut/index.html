Nut

Egyptian | Goddess of the Sky

PASSIVE

Flux

While Strength exceeds Intelligence, Ability hits grant Stellar Flux which provides Attack Speed

  • Otherwise, Attacks grant Astral Flux. Abilities consume this for bonus Magical Damage and effects.
  • Stacks decay every 3 seconds

ABILITY

Convergence

Your Attacks fire two additional projectiles that deal Magical Damage. Nut always shoots at least once, but may fire up to an additional 4 shots by using stacks. Hitting an enemy with an Attack provides 1 stack, hitting an enemy god provides 2

  • Astral Flux: Consumes 4 stacks to cause targets to lose Protections
  • Unused stacks are refunded if Nut dies while firing
  • Start the match with 2 full shots of this ability
  • If you cancel this ability without firing a shot, it goes on a 1 second cooldown
  • This ability passes through and damages all enemies, but stops on walls

ABILITY

Crashing Comet

Call down a comet that deals Magical Damage where it lands and less Magical Damage in an area around it

  • Astral Flux: Consumes 4 stacks to cause the comet to echo, damaging and trembling again
  • Enemies in the center are Rooted
  • Enemies on the edge are Trembled

ABILITY

Warp

Teleport in the direction you are moving and fire homing projectiles at nearby enemies that deal Magical Damage and applies a stacking slow. Subsequent hits deal 33% damage

  • Stellar Flux: Resets the Cooldown of Convergence and grants 8 Ammo
  • Astral Flux: Consumes 4 stacks to fire 2 additional projectiles
  • Projectiles deal increased damage to the first target hit, and reduced damage to subsequent hits

ABILITY

Skyfall

Become CC Immune and ascend into the sky. Fire a massive black hole that deals Magical Damage before detonating for additional Magical Damage and Pulling enemies towards the center

  • Grants 2 stacks of Flux for each god hit
  • Projectile lands after 1 second, and detonates 0.5 seconds later
