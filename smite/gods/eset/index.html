Eset

Egyptian | Goddess of Magic

PASSIVE

Funeral Rites

Whenever a god dies within 12.8m of Eset, she gains a stack of Funeral Rites; granting Health Regen and Mana Regen to her and nearby allies. Additionally, every third Attack from Eset is empowered; allowing it to hit in a wider radius and dealing Bonus Damage based on her stacks of Invocation

  • At 0 stacks, Eset's Invocation buff can stack up to 4 times
  • At 5 stacks, Eset's Invocation buff can stack up to 5 times
  • At 10 stacks, Eset's Invocation buff can stack up to 6 times, and Eset gains 10 Cooldown Rate

ABILITY

Wing Gust

Fire 4 projectiles in a line, each dealing Magical Damage to all targets in their path

  • While channeling, Eset gains Movement Speed, reduced backpedal and strafing penalties, and Knockback and Root immunity
  • After casting, Eset's next Attack will be empowered
  • Each projectile grants a stack of Invocation the first time it hits an enemy
  • This ability passes through and damages all enemies, and passes through walls

ABILITY

Spirit Ball

Send forth a ball of spirit energy. While it travels, Eset can re-activate this ability to detonate the ball, dealing Magical Damage and Stunning enemies

  • The further the ball travels, the higher its damage and Stun duration. Up to 1.5x the normal damage and Stun duration at max distance
  • When the ball deals damage to enemies, grants a stack of Invocation for each enemy hit
  • Deals 105/172.5/240/307.5/375 + 105% Intelligence Magical Damage, and Stuns for 1.5 seconds at max range
  • This ability passes through all enemies, and passes through walls

ABILITY

Dispel Magic

Eset Silences and Slows enemy gods at her target location. Enemy gods hit also have reduced Magical Protections

  • If Eset successfully hits an enemy god, allies near the Dispel Magic or Eset gain a Health Shield
  • If Eset hits multiple enemy gods, the Health Shield is increased by 30% for each enemy hit after the first up to a maximum of 60%
  • Applies up to a 80/160/240/320/400 Health Shield when hitting 3 enemy gods

ABILITY

Circle of Protection

Call down your staff and create a Circle of Protective Magic for 5 seconds before it explodes, dealing Magical Damage to all enemies and Healing all allies in the area

  • Eset can re-activate this ability at any time to detonate it, dealing the Magical Damage and Healing early
  • Any damage dealt in the area while it is active charges the circle, increasing its Magical Damage and Healing
  • While standing in the area, allies take reduced damage
  • Deals 360/483.75/625/784.75/960 + 100/112.5/125/137.5/150% Intelligence Magical Damage and 150/247.5/362.5/495/645 Healing at full charge

BASIC ATTACK

Eset Basic Attack

Fire a projectile that deals Magical Damage to the first enemy hit

  • Projectile stops on first target hit, and does not pass through walls
  • Has a 3 hit chain. Attacks in order of 1, 1, 1.5x damage and swing time. Final hit explodes
