For every 1 centimeter traveled, gain a 0.05% Damage Bonus on your next Attack

  • The distance traveled during Special Delivery and Sonic Boom contribute to the Damage Bonus

Made You Look

Move in the blink of an eye, dealing Physical Damage to every enemy within a target area

  • This ability can Critically Hit, apply Ability Item effects, and apply Attack Item effects

ABILITY

Maximum Velocity

Gain increased Attack Speed and become Slow Immune for the duration

  • Passively gain increased Movement Speed as this ability is ranked up

ABILITY

Special Delivery

Dash forward, Knocking aside minions and dealing Physical Damage to them. Grab the first enemy god encountered, Spinning them. The enemy god takes Physical Damage every 0.25 seconds until the effect ends. They are then Thrown in the direction you are facing

  • The Spin is treated as a Stun
  • This ability passes through and damages non-god enemies, but stops on walls and enemy gods

ABILITY

Sonic Boom

Gather energy for a powerful Dash. The range increases as you charge. Dash forward, dealing Physical Damage and Spinning enemies hit along the Dash. Gain Movement Speed after the Dash that scales based on the energy gathered

  • The Spin is treated as a Stun
  • You are CC Immune for the duration of the Dash

Mercury Basic Attack

  • If multiple enemies are in the area, the enemy closest to the center of the area will be hit
  • Has a 3 hit chain. Attacks in order of 1, 0.75, 1.25x damage and swing time
