Send a cat forward. If it hits an enemy, it Roots them and nearby enemies briefly and deals Physical Damage. The cat persists for a duration or until defeated, attacking nearby enemies for Physical Damage and applying a Slow

  • Cat is immune until it hits an enemy or reaches max range

Cat Call

Summon 3 spirit cats that follow you and attack nearby enemies for a duration. While they are active, you gain Strength

  • Cats cannot block movement and cannot attack structures
  • Cats Dash attack bleeding enemies, dealing Physical Damage and Slowing them
  • When you cast Razor Whip, each cat resets its Dash Cooldown and Dashes to the first enemy hit
  • When you cast Pounce, cats follow you through the Leap
