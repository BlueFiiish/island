Cu Chulainn

Celtic | The Hound of Ulster

PASSIVE

Berserk

Your Mana, Mana Regen, and Mana Heals convert into your Health Pool instead of Mana. Abilities cost Rage, which only regenerates up to 25. After building high Rage, you go Berserk for a duration, gaining Strength and a Health Shield. While Berserk, your Abilities transform and cost no Rage

  • Gain Rage by hitting Abilities, per Attack, and when you or a nearby ally takes damage
  • Attacks and Abilities must deal at least 1 damage to generate Rage
  • Rage resets to 25 when you enter Berserk or respawn
  • The transformation is canceled if you lose the required Rage during the transformation phase

ABILITY

Barbed Spear

Thrust your spear forward, dealing Physical Damage to enemies in front of you and Stunning weaker minions. Barbs reduce their Healing received for a duration

  • Reduces enemy Healing
  • This ability Stuns lane minions and jungle monsters, but not gods or jungle bosses
  • Hitting a minion restores Rage, more for a god, up to a cap
  • This ability passes through and damages all enemies, but stops on walls

ABILITY

Vent Anger

Vent superheated steam for a duration, gaining Movement Speed and dealing Physical Damage to nearby enemies repeatedly

  • This ability can be used without being ranked
  • This ability drains your Rage and returns none
  • This ability damages all enemies in the area, but stops on walls

ABILITY

Salmon's Leap

Leap to a target location and slam down, dealing Physical Damage to enemies around and in front of you.

  • Hitting a minion restores Rage, more for a god, up to a cap
  • This ability passes through and damages all enemies, and passes through walls

ABILITY

Spear of Mortal Pain

Swing Gae Bulg in a circle around you, dealing Physical Damage and Knocking Up all nearby enemies. You are CC Immune while channeling

  • Hitting a minion restores Rage, more for a god, up to a cap
  • This ability damages all enemies in the area, but stops on walls

BASIC ATTACK

Cu Chulainn Basic Attack

Deal Physical Damage to an enemy in front of you

  • If multiple enemies are in the area, the enemy closest to the center of the area will be hit
  • Human Form: Has a 3 hit chain. Attacks in order of 0.7, 0.7, 1x damage and swing time
  • Berserk Form: Has a 3 hit chain. Attacks in order of 1.05, 1.05, 1.5x damage and swing time
