Sylvanus

Roman | Keeper of the Wild

PASSIVE

Nature's Bounty

When your abilities hit or are deployed, a seed pod appears. Picking it up restores Mana and reduces your cooldowns. Picking up 8 spawns a pod near Sylvanus

  • Reduces cooldowns by 0.5 seconds; at level 15 the reduction increases to 1 second

ABILITY

Verdant Growth

Throw a pod that deals Magical Damage, applies Protection Reduction, and Roots enemies hit

  • If it lands on the ground without hitting an enemy, it grows into a Flower after 6 seconds that grants nearby allies Mana Regen
  • The pod can be destroyed by Attacks, applying Protection Reduction to the destroyer
  • Grown Flowers wilt for 6 seconds when destroyed, allowing them to still interact with Wisps and Nature's Grasp

ABILITY

Wisps

Release Wisps that restores Health to allies or deals Magical Damage to enemies repeatedly over time. Allies gain Protections while healed

  • The first Wisp tick deals twice the normal amount of Healing and Damage
  • Wisps can revive Wilted Flowers or cause Living Flowers to grant their Protections in an aura
  • Healing and Damage are reduced by 65% when affecting Minions. Seeds are not spawned when hitting Minions

ABILITY

Nature's Grasp

Extend a vine that Stuns the first enemy god hit and Pulls them toward you

  • Sylvanus can pull Pods to him, granting a Mana Regen aura for 30 seconds. If Sylvanus drops below 40% Health, he consumes the Aura to gain Wisps
  • This ability stops on first enemy god hit, and passes through walls

ABILITY

Wrath of Terra

Slam the ground, Knocking Up enemies in an area and dealing Magical Damage repeatedly over time

  • If a Pod has been grabbed, consume it to apply Verdant Growth's Protection Reduction
  • Deals damage 6 times over 5 seconds

BASIC ATTACK

Sylvanus Basic Attack

Lob a projectile at a target location that explodes, dealing Magical Damage to all enemies

  • Projectile can be thrown over walls
