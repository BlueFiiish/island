Achilles

Greek | Hero of the Trojan War

PASSIVE

Gift of the Gods

Choose to wear armor or forgo it. Wearing armor grants bonus Health and Protections, while forgoing it grants bonus Strength and Movement Speed

  • Swap between armor states by using Basic Attacks while the Passive targeter is active inside the Fountain
  • Your chosen armor state also adds effects to Radiant Glory and Combat Dodge

ABILITY

Shield of Achilles

Punch forward with your shield, dealing Physical Damage and Stunning enemies in a short cone. The force of the shield radiates further, dealing reduced damage

  • The radiated force deals reduced Physical Damage
  • Non-god targets take increased Physical Damage
  • Increased Stun duration when armored, increased Scaling when forgoing your armor

ABILITY

Radiant Glory

You are blessed by the gods, gaining bonus Strength, Protections, and Crowd Control Reduction for a short duration. Damaging enemies with abilities during this time Heals you

  • When armored, the gods' blessings also grant you a Physical Shield
  • When forgoing your armor, hitting an enemy god with your attacks from behind reduces their Protections
  • You may only Heal up to 4 times per ability hit

ABILITY

Combat Dodge (Spear Strike)

Dodge in your current direction and ready your spear to strike enemies for Physical Damage. Hitting an enemy god with the strike allows you to recast the ability once before it goes on cooldown

  • When armored, striking an enemy god with your spear Slows them, stacking up to 2 times
  • When forgoing your armor, the speed of your Dodge is doubled
  • You can use Abilities and Basic Attacks between Dodges
  • After readying your spear, you have 4 seconds to strike before the ability ends

ABILITY

Fatal Strike

Prepare briefly, then Dash foward, dealing Physical Damage to enemies and Executing gods that are low health. Killing a god allows you to recast this ability up to 5 times

  • Each kill exposes your heel, increasing your damage taken
  • The dash ends early upon hitting an enemy god
  • You are CC Immune during the preparation phase, and while Dashing
  • The preparation before the Dash lasts 1 second
  • This ability passes through and damages non-god enemies, but stops on walls and enemy gods

BASIC ATTACK

Achilles Basic Attack

Deal Physical Damage to an enemy in front of you

  • If multiple enemies are in the area, the enemy closest to the center of the area will be hit
