Danzaburou

Japanese | The Legendary Tanuki

PASSIVE

Dishonest Duplication

You have a chance of spawning a clone of yourself with each Basic Attack hitting an enemy

  • Clones last 3 seconds, deal 10% of your Basic Attack damage, and take 300% increased damage
  • Clones have a 5% + 0.25% Per Level chance of spawning a clone
  • Note: This feature is bugged, as clones are unable to spawn additional clones
  • Clones target the enemy that was hit when they were created

ABILITY

Fool's Gold

Roll out 3 money bags that deal Physical Damage to enemies hit and then explode dealing Physical Damage

  • Bags stop on enemy gods, Trembling them and dropping a coin
  • Coins can be picked up by yourself or allied gods to provide you 10 gold
  • Subsequent hits of bags or explosions on the same enemy deal 15% damage
  • This ability passes through and damages non-god enemies, and bounces off walls, but stops on enemy gods

ABILITY

Alluring Spirits

Drink some sake, Healing over time before throwing the bottle, dealing Physical Damage to enemies hit

  • On landing, the bottle applies a stacking Slow to enemy gods every 0.25 seconds
  • On reaching 3 stacks, they are Taunted towards the bottle
  • This ability does 40% bonus damage to Jungle Monsters
  • The inner circle deals damage on landing while the entire area slows
  • The bottle can be broken early with enemy Attacks
  • Taunt is not affected by Diminishing Returns
  • If the Taunted enemy takes more than 30% of their Max Health, the Taunt ends early

ABILITY

Tanuki Trickery

Create a field where you gain Movement Speed, Slow Immunity, your Attack Movement Penalty is removed, and your Dishonest Duplication chance is doubled. When you leave the area, it disappears and you transform into a leaf while decoys mimic your movement

  • Enemies in the field are Slowed
  • As a leaf you gain increased Movement Speed, Slow Immunity, and a buff that causes your next Basic Attack to deal Physical Damage
  • Taking damage or attacking while in leaf form will end it early, however there is a brief delay before this can happen on first transforming

ABILITY

Uproarious Rocket

Fire a rocket that locks onto enemy gods or reactive this ability to become the rocket! The rocket deals Physical Damage, Stuns the first enemy god hit, and explodes dealing Physical Damage

  • You are Rooted and CC Immune while Channeling, and can only aim within 180 degrees
  • You are immune to damage while in rocket form
  • The rocket travels for a maximum of 4 seconds
  • You can cancel this ability while a rocket to transform back, sending the rocket directly ahead
  • This ability passes through and damages non-god enemies, but stops on walls and enemy gods
