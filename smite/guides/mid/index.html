Mid (Mage)

Magical burst/sustained damage. Mid lane — controls center of the map.

Guide revised 2026-04-11 3 sources

Read this with the date in mind

This guide was last revised on 2026-04-11. The item and build data on the rest of the site is synced to OB42 (2026-08-27), so any tier list or patch note below may be older than the numbers on the item and build pages.

Mid laners provide magical damage, zone control, and burst potential. They farm mid lane, rotate for jungle camps and ganks, and provide AoE damage in teamfights.

Standard Build Template

For traditional ability-based mages:

Slot Item Purpose
Starter Conduit Gem → Archmage's Gem or Pendulum Of The Ages Mana sustain + scaling
1 Book of Thoth Highest Intelligence once stacked (~116 total)
2 Gem of Focus 20% CDR + Momentum MS stacks (nerfed OB32: 75→65 INT)
3 Spear of Desolation 100 Intelligence + kill-reset CDR
4 Rod of Tahuti +25% Intelligence from items — multiplicative
5 Obsidian Shard 35% magical penetration
6 Flex: Staff of Myrddin / Totem of Death / Chronos' Pendant / Blinking Abyss
Relic Purification Beads or Aegis of Acceleration

Penetration Ordering

  1. Totem of Death (slot 3-4) — stacking protection shred (6% per stack, max 30%). Applied first
  2. Obsidian Shard (slot 5-6) — 35% pen calculated on the already-reduced protections

Totem first, Shard second = maximum effective penetration.

Anti-Heal

Divine Ruin — 85 Intelligence + 25% healing reduction on damage. Slot 3-4, replaces Spear of Desolation when enemy has healers or heavy lifesteal.

Key Mechanics

  • Book of Thoth first → one-shot wave by level 7-8 → rotation window to gank or take camps
  • Gem of Focus second → CDR + MS for faster rotations
  • Burst mages (Zeus, Scylla, Vulcan): Stack raw Intelligence
  • Sustained mages (Anubis, Hecate): Prioritize CDR + lifesteal (Bancroft's Talon)
  • CDR thresholds: 20% (Gem alone), 30-40% (Gem + Spear/Chronos'), 40-50% (triple CDR)
