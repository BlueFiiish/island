Solo (Bruiser/Warrior)

Frontline bruiser. Solo lane — sustain, anti-heal, dive/peel in teamfights.

Guide revised 2026-04-11 3 sources

Read this with the date in mind

This guide was last revised on 2026-04-11. The item and build data on the rest of the site is synced to OB42 (2026-08-27), so any tier list or patch note below may be older than the numbers on the item and build pages.

Solo laners are the team's frontline threat. They sustain through 1v1 laning, carry anti-heal for the team, and either dive enemy backlines or peel for their own carry in teamfights.

Build Patterns

Damage-Leaning Bruiser (2 damage, 4 defense)

Shield Splitter → Brawler's Beat Stick → defense from slot 3 on

Use when: team already has tanky support, you need to be a damage threat.

Tank-Leaning Bruiser (0-1 damage, 5-6 defense)

Wyrmskin Hide or Contagion first → hold damage or single Shield Splitter at slot 4

Use when: team lacks frontline, you are primary engage.

CDR Trio

Glorious Pridwen (12%) + Genji's Guard (CDR on magic damage taken) + Spirit Robe

Common on Mordred, Achilles, Sun Wukong for tanky CDR stacking.

Anti-Heal Stacking

  • Brawler's Beat Stick + Contagion = stack (offensive + defensive aura)
  • Contagion + Pestilence = do NOT stack (both are aura anti-heal)
  • Build one aura + one application for maximum reduction

Key Mechanics

  • Sustain wins lanes — whoever sustains longer controls farm
  • Anti-heal is your job — always build at least one anti-heal item
  • Dive survivability: Spirit Robe (CC reduction), Hide of the Nemean Lion (reflect autos), Spectral Armor (anti-crit)
  • Split-push: Apollo with Aspect of Harmony + Golden Blade is the premier split-pusher; ult allows global rotation back

OB32 Changes

  • Hercules nerfed: base prots reduced, cooldowns increased → dropped to C tier
  • Gilgamesh added: quest-based warrior, still settling (B tier)
  • Spectral Armor buffed: 400→450 HP, crit reduction shares with allies
  • Pharaoh's Curse buffed: +250 HP added
  • Radiant Bulwark buffed: removed self-slow, +20% damage mitigation while channeling
