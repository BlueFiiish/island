Support role

Support (Guardian/Warrior)

Frontline peel and utility. Duo lane with [[Carry]].

Guide revised 2026-09-11 3 sources

Supports protect the carry in lane, rotate for objectives, provide CC and auras in teamfights, and ward the map. The current meta favors supports that can both initiate AND peel, not pure defensive play — Horus and Xing Tian head the OB43 pool and are also the two most-banned gods in the game.

OB43 Tier List

Sources: smitebrain.com/gods?role=support (Open Beta 43, ranked Conquest, Obsidian+, read 2026-09-11) for the tier letters and win rates, cross-checked against smitesource.com/gods (live all-role win-rate leaderboard, read 2026-09-11). Tier letters are SmiteBrain's own. Its win rates are not centred on 50% - it samples one tracked high-MMR team per match, so read the letter, not the percentage. Only the top 22 of 70 rows in the pool were captured, so gods below C/D may simply not be listed here rather than being unranked.

Both sources agree on the top of the pool: smitesource.com has Horus 4th of all 91 (57.8%), Xing Tian 7th (56.9%) and Geb 9th (56.3%). Horus and Xing Tian are also the two most-banned gods in the game on smitesource (75.6% and 78.9% ban rate). They disagree on Ares (14th overall on smitesource, C here).

Note the OB43 item changes land hardest on this role: Rod of Asclepius was redesigned into a healing-amp passive, Chandra's Grace and Heartwood Charm were rebuilt then hotfix-nerfed, Eros' Bow became a buff item, and Soul Locket is new.

Patch history: the OB32 list this replaced described a meta favouring supports that could both initiate and peel; that read still holds, with Horus and Xing Tian as its clearest expression.

Core Items

Tier 1 — Build every game:

  • Selflessness → Heroism: Default starter. Shield 50 + 10% max HP on ally Hard CC, 15s CD
  • Gauntlet of Thebes: 900 HP at full stacks. Rush and stack early via assists
  • Spirit Robe: 20/20 prots, 20 Tenacity, +40/+40 prots + 7% HP heal on Hard CC

Tier 2 — Build most games:

  • Stampede: 30 phys prots, 250 HP. Active: slow immunity + 60% MS for allies. Essential for rotations/disengage
  • Stygian Anchor: 35/30 dual prots, 25% healing reduction on damage. Primary support anti-heal
  • Genji's Guard: Magical prots + CDR

Tier 3 — Situational:

  • Screeching Gargoyle: 50 magical prots, 15% CDR, active 1s silence + 10% prot shred
  • Glorious Pridwen: 20/20 prots, 12% CDR, shield on ult = 125% of prots, explodes for AoE
  • Spectral Armor: Anti-crit vs crit carries
  • Breastplate of Valor: Physical prots + CDR
  • Contagion: Alternative anti-heal (better when you're being focused — stacks on damage taken)

Anti-Heal

  • Stygian Anchor and Contagion do NOT stack — pick one per build
  • Cerberus passive anti-heal stacks WITH item anti-heal
  • Sobek's Sickening Strike has built-in anti-heal

Warding

  • 2 regular Wards + 1 Sentry Ward at a time
  • Early (1-15 min): Ward duo-side jungle entrances, Sentry on Gold Fury
  • Mid/Late: Transition wards to Fire Giant side, Sentry the objective pit
  • Buy wards on every back — non-negotiable

Decision Framework

  1. Carry partner: Hunter carry → peel items. Mage carry → Screeching Gargoyle for magical prot aura
  2. Enemy dive: Heavy dive → rush Spirit Robe earlier, Phantom Shell first relic
  3. Enemy healing: Any healer/lifesteal → build Stygian Anchor or Contagion
  4. Team damage type: Physical-heavy team → your magical prot auras matter more
  5. Enemy CC level: High CC → Spirit Robe mandatory, consider Purification Beads
