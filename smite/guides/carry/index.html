Carry (Hunter/ADC)

Physical sustained damage dealer. Duo lane with [[Support]].

Guide revised 2026-04-11 3 sources

Read this with the date in mind

This guide was last revised on 2026-04-11. The item and build data on the rest of the site is synced to OB42 (2026-08-27), so any tier list or patch note below may be older than the numbers on the item and build pages.

The Carry role provides sustained physical DPS through basic attacks. Carries farm in duo lane early, scale through items, and become the primary damage threat in late-game teamfights.

Core Items

Build every game:

  • Devourer's Gauntlet — lifesteal stacking, rush first in most builds
  • Odysseus' Bow — chain lightning + 45% attack speed, core waveclear
  • Titan's Bane — 35% pen (Shattering), mandatory anti-tank

Build most games:

  • Dagger of Frenzy — attack speed + damage steroid active, resets on god kill
  • Qin's Blade — %HP on-hit, anti-tank DPS
  • Tyrfing — early-mid power spike with stacking passive

Build Paths

On-Hit / Penetration (Default — safe into any comp)

Devourer's Gauntlet → Tyrfing or Dagger of Frenzy → Qin's Blade → Titan's Bane → Odysseus' Bow

Not countered by a single defensive item. Consistent DPS against all targets.

Crit (Into squishy comps)

Devourer's Gauntlet → Rage → Deathbringer → Demon Blade or flex

Explosive burst against squishies but countered by Spectral Armor (-30% crit reduction, buffed OB32).

Key Mechanics

  • Crit multiplier: Base 1.65x in SMITE 2 (not 2.0x). Deathbringer raises to 2.0x
  • Pen stacking: Titan's Bane and Obsidian Shard do NOT stack — only 35% pen total
  • Flat pen applies before % pen — Oath-Sworn Spear + Titan's Bane is effective combo
  • Anti-heal options: Brawler's Beat Stick, Avenging Blade (prot shred vs healers), Qin's Blade

Relic

Purification Beads is the default carry relic in OB32. Aegis of Acceleration is secondary vs burst dive.
